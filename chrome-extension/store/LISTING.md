**Detailed description**

```
Read any web page faster, right where you are. Press Alt+Shift+S and BriskRead opens a clean reader over the page you are on. No copy and paste, no new tab, no account.

SIX WAYS TO READ
• Flash: one word at a time at a fixed focal point, so your eyes stop jumping across lines.
• Focus: the full text with a highlight that moves at your pace.
• Phrase and Flow: short word groups, or the current word inside its sentence.
• Bionic: bold word openings that guide the eye.
• Page: clean, distraction-free text.

LISTEN WHILE YOU READ
Turn on Listen and the highlight follows the voice. Read aloud is free at any speed and uses the natural voices already in your browser. If a voice stops responding, BriskRead keeps reading silently so you never lose your place.

BIONIC READING ON THE PAGE ITSELF
Alt+Shift+K bolds the opening of every word on the live page, including content that loads as you scroll. Press it again and the page returns exactly as it was.

PDFS TOO
Open a PDF in Chrome and press Alt+Shift+S. You can also open PDFs from your computer, or paste any text.

READ LATER, AND PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Alt+Shift+L saves a page to your read-later queue. BriskRead remembers your position in every article.

AI SUMMARY AND QUESTIONS (OPTIONAL)
Add your own free Gemini API key to get a bullet-point summary or ask questions about the text. Text goes to Google only when you press Summarise or Ask.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Reads a page only when you ask it to.
• Page text stays in your browser. No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads in the extension.
• Your settings and read-later queue stay on your device.

MADE FOR LONG READING
Speed from 100 to 1,200 words per minute, a gentle slow start, dark, light and sepia themes, three text sizes, and full keyboard control: Space to play, arrows to skip and change speed, 1 to 6 to switch modes, Esc to close.

Works on articles, blogs, newsletters, documentation and most web pages. Chrome does not allow extensions on its own pages (such as chrome:// pages and the Chrome Web Store). On those, BriskRead lets you paste the text instead.

Free, from briskread.com.
```

## Privacy practices tab

**Single purpose description**

```
BriskRead helps people read the web page they are on faster. When the user invokes it, it shows that page's text in a speed-reading view (one word at a time, a moving highlight, bionic formatting or read aloud), and it can save pages to read later.
```

**Permission justifications**

| Permission | Justification to paste |
| --- | --- |
| activeTab | Reads the text of the current tab only after the user presses a BriskRead shortcut, clicks the toolbar button or chooses a BriskRead right-click option. BriskRead has no access to other tabs or to pages in the background. |
| scripting | After the user invokes BriskRead on a tab, injects the reader view, extracts that page's article text, applies bionic formatting on request, and reads the bytes of a PDF open in Chrome's PDF viewer so it can be shown in the reader. All injected code is bundled in the package. |
| storage | Saves the user's reading settings (speed, mode, theme, voice), the read-later queue, reading positions and the optional Gemini API key locally on the device. Nothing is synced or sent to a server. |
| contextMenus | Adds right-click options: Speed read this page, Speed read selection, Speed read linked page, Bionic reading on this page, and Save to read later. |

There are no host permissions to justify.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code. (pdf.js is bundled in `vendor/pdfjs/`; the only network call is the optional Gemini API request, which returns text, not code.)

**Data usage: what to tick**

- Tick **Website content**. Reason: if the user turns on AI summary or questions with their own key, the text of the page they are reading is sent from their browser to Google's Gemini API. The developer never receives it.
- Leave everything else unticked. Page text read for speed reading is processed only in the browser. The read-later queue (titles, URLs, text) and settings never leave the device. There is no analytics, no account and no tracking in the extension.
- Tick all three certifications: not sold to third parties; not used or transferred for purposes unrelated to the single purpose; not used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
